# HurricaneMap — Logo prompts

Five prompt variants for an AI image generator. Each must produce a transparent PNG (RGBA), no background tile, no drop shadow on the background. Square 1024×1024 output, then export 512×512 + 256×256 sizes.

Output filenames once selected:
- `branding/logo.png` (square, 512×512)
- `branding/banner.png` (wide, 1280×320)
- `branding/favicon.png` (square, 64×64)

Color palette (Catppuccin Mocha):
- Base background: `#11111b`
- Accent gradient: `#cba6f7` (mauve) → `#74c7ec` (sapphire)
- Highlight: `#cdd6f4` (text)

## 1 — Minimal

> A minimalist logo of a stylized hurricane / tropical-cyclone spiral, two thin curving arms forming a yin-yang-like swirl with a small filled eye in the center. Single weight, monochrome white-to-mauve gradient (#cdd6f4 → #cba6f7). Geometric, flat, no drop shadows. Centered on a fully transparent background. Vector-clean edges. Square 1024×1024.

## 2 — App icon

> An iOS / Android app icon depicting a hurricane spiral viewed from above, three swirling arms in a clockwise vortex, soft inner glow at the eye. Color: linear gradient from sapphire (#74c7ec) at outer arms to mauve (#cba6f7) at the eye, on a deep navy rounded-rect base (#11111b) with subtle 1px highlight on the top edge. Squircle / rounded-rect canvas. No text. 1024×1024 PNG, transparent corners.

## 3 — Wordmark

> Wordmark logo "HurricaneMap" in a tight modern sans-serif (think Inter Bold or Geist Bold), letterspacing tight. The letter "O" replaced with a tiny hurricane spiral icon in mauve→sapphire gradient. White-to-text-color body (#cdd6f4). Single line, lockup proportioned for a 4:1 horizontal banner. Transparent background. 1280×320 PNG.

## 4 — Emblem

> Circular badge / emblem logo. Outer ring contains the text "HURRICANE MAP · 1851 - PRESENT" arched. Center: a stylized hurricane spiral over a faint compass rose. Single-color line work in mauve (#cba6f7), thin strokes, vintage cartographic aesthetic. Transparent background. 1024×1024 PNG.

## 5 — Abstract

> Abstract topographical / radar-sweep illustration: concentric pressure-isobar curves spiraling inward toward a small bright point, suggestive of a satellite IR loop of a major hurricane. Limited palette: sapphire (#74c7ec) outer rings fading through peach (#fab387) into pink (#f38ba8) into mauve (#cba6f7) at the eye. Square framing. Transparent background. 1024×1024 PNG.
