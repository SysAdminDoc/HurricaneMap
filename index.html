    About HurricaneMap

    Every dot on this map is a recorded U.S. hurricane or tropical-storm landfall, drawn directly from NOAA's HURDAT2 Atlantic and Eastern North Pacific best-track databases — the same data the National Hurricane Center uses for its post-season analyses.

    How landfalls are detected

    • Explicit: HURDAT2 records flagged with the L identifier (the official landfall marker — center of the cyclone crossing a coastline).
    • Inferred: For storms without an L marker but whose 6-hourly track passes over U.S. land while at tropical-storm or hurricane intensity (e.g. Iniki 1992 on Kauai). Landfall position interpolated when the crossing happens between synoptic times.

    Known gaps in the source data

    • 1971-1990: HURDAT2 only marks continental-U.S. landfalls for 1851-1970 and 1991-onward. Some 1971-1990 landfalls are picked up by inferred-landfall detection but a few will be missing from this database.
    • Pre-aircraft (before 1944) and pre-satellite (before late-1960s) eras under-sample storms and bias intensities low.
    • Wind radii and radius-of-maximum-wind only available from 2004 and 2021 onward respectively.

    Archived NEXRAD radar (📡)

    Every storm from August 1995 onward ships with the full set of in-coverage 6-hourly radar frames baked into the repo (~512 MB across 1700+ PNGs in 139 storm folders). Two ways to use it:

    • Direct: click 📡 next to any landfall in the storm panel. Steppers walk frame-by-frame; loop animates the storm's full U.S. radar timeline (Katrina '05 = 22 frames over five days), with the map auto-panning to follow the eye.
    • In track animation: when "Play track animation" is on, a 📡 checkbox in the control bar binds the radar overlay to the simulated UTC clock — as the spinning glyph travels the path, the actual NEXRAD reflectivity paints onto the map at exactly the right moment. Toggle off to hide.

    Coverage:

    • CONUS landfalls — base reflectivity (n0r) at 5-minute cadence (hourly for the 1995-2002 window).
    • Hawaii / Puerto Rico — composite (n0q), 2010-onward.
    • Pre-1995 — no radar archive available.
    • Frames not in the local archive transparently fall back to live IEM URLs.
