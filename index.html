    About HurricaneMap

    Every dot is a recorded U.S. hurricane or tropical-storm landfall from NOAA HURDAT2.

    How landfalls are detected

    • Explicit: HURDAT2 records flagged with the L identifier.
    • Inferred: Tropical tracks that cross U.S. land without an L marker.

    Known gaps in the source data

    • HURDAT2 has a 1971-1990 continental landfall-marker gap.
    • Early observing eras under-sample storms.
    • Wind radii are available only in recent years.

    Archived NEXRAD radar (📡)

    Storms from August 1995 onward include archived radar.

    • Direct: select 📡 beside a landfall.
    • Track animation: synchronize radar to the simulated clock.

    Coverage:

    • Continental U.S. uses n0r reflectivity.
    • Hawaii and Puerto Rico use n0q reflectivity.
    • No radar archive is available before 1995.
    • Missing local frames fall back to live IEM URLs.

    Data: NOAA NHC HURDAT2. Radar: Iowa State IEM. Maps: CartoDB / OpenStreetMap.
